# Recording guide — what to capture from the reference games

**For the owner. Written 18 Nov 2026.** Each request below unblocks a specific task in `Revision_Plan_ModelOnReferences.md`. Do them in the order listed; the first two matter most. You do not have to do all of them.

## How to record (applies to every request)

- **Record the screen, not the phone's camera.** iPhone: swipe down for Control Center and tap the round record button. Stop it from the same place or the red status bar.
- **Go slowly.** Pause about one second before every tap and one second after. Tap the same spot firmly. The recording does not show your finger, so slow, deliberate taps let the analysis tell what caused what.
- **Say it out loud as you go if you like, but write the numbers down too.** The analysis reads pictures, not sound. Prices, timers and counts must be visible on screen at some moment, held for at least two seconds.
- **Keep clips short**, under three minutes each. One request, one clip.
- **Screenshots are fine where the request says "screenshots"** (a screenshot is steadier than a video frame).
- **Where to put the files:** the `Screen Recordings` folder next to the project, as before. Name each file with the request id, for example `R1_TravelTown_order_partial.mov`, so it can be found.
- **Do not spend money you do not want to spend.** Wherever a request involves a purchase, stop at the confirmation screen, capture it, and back out unless you are happy to buy.
- **Which game, which version:** note the version number shown in the game's settings in your message to me once, per game.

## R1 — Travel Town and Gossip Harbor: filling orders (most important)

**Unblocks:** W1 (the order rule), task 1.8 (the buy button on a missing item). **Both games, about 3 minutes each.**

Find a time when an order needs two or three items and you are holding only some of them. Then record:

1. The whole screen with the order card visible and **one item held, the others missing.** Hold it still for three seconds. I want to confirm there is no button.
2. Tap the **empty slot** of a missing item (in Travel Town, the one with the red cart badge). Show what happens. **Stop at the price screen**, hold it for three seconds, then back out.
3. Make or buy the last missing item so the card is complete. Show the card **just before** and **just after** it turns complete. I am looking at the exact colour change and when the button appears.
4. Hold **two copies** of a needed item on the board (a spare one). Press the button and show **which one leaves the board.**
5. If you can, have **two orders asking for the same item** while you hold only one. Show what the cards look like and what happens when you press the button on one.
6. Show the board and the order card a few seconds **after** delivery: the cleared tile, the new order that appears, the rewards flying out.

Tell me in your message: whether the delivery button is on the card or somewhere else, and what you see happen to the tint colours.

## R2 — Gossip Harbor: the wildcard and ready-made items

**Unblocks:** the wildcard question in 1.1. **About 2 minutes.**

If either game has an item that stands in for any other item (a wildcard or joker), record using it on an order, and record what happens to it. If neither does, say so; that is also an answer.

## R3 — Both games: what skipping a wait costs

**Unblocks:** W3 (skip price). **Screenshots, both games.**

Find six or more waits of different lengths (a generator cooling down, a building timer, anything with a "skip" or "speed up" button). For each one take a screenshot that shows **the time remaining and the price on the skip button together.** Aim for a spread: nearly done, about half, and the full wait. Come back to the same timer an hour later if you can and screenshot it again; that is the most useful pair of all.

## R4 — Both games: the first ten minutes (optional)

**Unblocks:** W4 (tutorial). **Needs a fresh start, so only do this if you can without losing a real save** (a second device, a new account, or a reinstall you do not mind). Gossip Harbor's first ten minutes I have already measured on a fresh profile; Travel Town's I have only from its files. If you can only do one, do Travel Town. Record from the first launch to the point the game stops hand-holding. Do not skip any popup; tap slowly.

## R5 — Gossip Harbor: chores

**Unblocks:** 9.1 (chores). **About 5 minutes, once chores are unlocked in your save.**

Open the chores screen and hold it for three seconds. Then: (1) screenshot every chore with its cost, reward and XP, (2) complete one chore, recording the whole thing, (3) write down how many chores a day you see and whether the list refreshes.

## R6 — Both games: a first-time discovery

**Unblocks:** 9.2 (discovery collection). **About 2 minutes each.**

Create an item **you have never made before** (a new highest tier in some chain). Record the moment of creation and the 10 seconds after. I want to know whether any reward, popup or counter appears. Then open the collection or chain viewer for that chain and show the new item in it. If you have to pick between games, do Gossip Harbor.

## R7 — Gossip Harbor: live events hub

**Unblocks:** 7.3 (event cadence). **About 4 minutes.**

Open the events area from the main screen. Scroll slowly through **every event card**, holding each for two seconds so the timer, the reward list and the progress steps are readable. Note how many events are running at once.

## R8 — Both games: store offers, three times

**Unblocks:** 9.3 (contextual offers). **Screenshots.**

Open the store or any special-offer popup **three times on different days** and screenshot every offer with its price and contents. I am looking at whether the offer changes with what you have, not whether it is good.

## R9 — Gossip Harbor: a story scene (only if you want a story layer)

**Unblocks:** 9.4 (story and characters). **About 5 minutes.**

Play from a story scene's start to its end. I want to know how often dialogue appears and whether it blocks play.

## When something goes wrong

If a recording will not save, or the game behaves differently from what a request assumes, **do not force it.** Tell me what you saw instead. A different behaviour is information, not a failed recording.
